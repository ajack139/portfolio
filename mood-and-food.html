Mood & Food

A wellness concept that helps students recognize emotional eating patterns by logging mood + meals and reflecting on triggers with lightweight, user-friendly prompts.

Figma User Research Prototyping Usability Testing
← Back to Projects

Problem

Many students stress-eat without noticing patterns. Existing wellness apps can feel overwhelming or too “clinical,” so users don’t stick with them.

Goal

Make mood + food reflection simple enough to do daily, so users can spot triggers and build healthier habits.

My role

  • Conducted user research + synthesis
  • Designed flows + wireframes
  • Built interactive prototype
  • Tested + iterated based on feedback

Constraints

  • Course project timeline (prototype only)
  • No real backend or long-term usage data
  • Self-reported inputs (mood + meals)

Process

1) Research

I gathered insights from surveys/interviews and identified common triggers (stress, boredom, social pressure).

  • What users struggle with: consistency + clarity
  • What users want: quick logging + supportive tone

2) Prototype

I mapped core flows and designed a simple “daily check-in” that connects mood → meal → reflection.

  • Mood check-in → meal log → reflection prompt
  • Minimal screens, clear CTA buttons, friendly language

3) Test & iterate

Surveys were used to gether insight about user sentiment around

  • Simplified labels and reduced the number of steps
  • Improved navigation and made progress more visible

Results / what I learned

  • Users complete logs more often when the flow is “one minute or less.”
  • Neutral wording matters—people avoid features that feel judgey.
  • Users wanted small wins (streaks/insights) without feeling pressured.

Next steps

  • Add gentle reminders + streaks (optional)
  • Expand nutrition database + shortcuts
  • Wearable integration for stress/activity signals
  • Longer-term testing (habit formation)
