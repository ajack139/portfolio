Mood & Food

A wellness concept that helps students recognize emotional eating patterns by logging mood + meals and reflecting on triggers with lightweight, user-friendly prompts.

Figma User Research Prototyping Usability Testing
← Back to Projects

Problem

Many students stress‑eat without noticing patterns. Existing wellness apps can feel overwhelming or overly clinical, reducing long‑term engagement.

Goal

Make mood + food reflection simple enough to do daily, so users can spot triggers and build healthier habits.

What I did

  • Conducted user research + synthesis
  • Designed flows + wireframes
  • Built an interactive prototype
  • Tested + iterated based on feedback

Tools

  • Figma (wireframes + prototype)
  • Surveys/interviews for research
  • Usability testing + iteration notes

Process

1) Research

Gathered insights and identified common triggers (stress, boredom, social pressure).

  • Users struggle with consistency + clarity
  • Users want quick logging + supportive tone

2) Prototype

Mapped a simple “daily check‑in” flow that connects mood → meal → reflection.

  • Minimal screens + clear CTA buttons
  • Friendly language and easy navigation

3) Test & iterate

Used post‑test surveys to find friction points, then simplified labels and reduced steps.

  • Improved navigation and made progress more visible
  • Reduced cognitive load during check‑in

Key screens from the prototype.

What I learned

  • Users complete logs more often when the flow is “one minute or less.”
  • Neutral wording matters—people avoid features that feel judgmental.
  • Small wins (streaks/insights) help motivation without pressure.
